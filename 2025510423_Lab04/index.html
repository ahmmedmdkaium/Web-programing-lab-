Welcome to the Court

Badminton Club welcomes students who enjoy fast rallies, friendly teamwork and healthy exercise. We train on campus courts, practise serves and footwork, and play relaxed matches every week. Beginners are as welcome as experienced players, because every member learns by playing together and helping each other improve.

Benefits of Joining

  • Better stamina, speed and hand-eye coordination from regular rallies.
  • Coaching tips from senior players on grip, serve and footwork.
  • Shared rackets and shuttlecocks for beginners during training.
  • New friends from every faculty and a chance to play in friendly matches.

Come for one rally, stay for the team!

How to Join

Any current ECUT student may join, and no experience is needed. Email the organiser, Li Wei, at badminton.club@example.edu or simply come to our first meeting on Monday at 18:00 in Sports Hall Court 1. Bring sports shoes, a water bottle and a towel.
